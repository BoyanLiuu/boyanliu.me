Movie Passport puts world cinema on a 3D globe. Countries with films are lit in silver, and posters stand on them like small framed prints. Data comes from TMDB; it is a non-commercial project.

## What it does

- **Browse by country:** each country lists the films it made and the ones it co-produced, with counts and decades. A country with no films suggests nearby ones that do.
- **Open a film:** the camera flies to it and every production country lights up. The details page adds cast and crew, the trailer (loaded from YouTube only when you press play), where to stream, rent or buy it in your region (JustWatch data), and similar films.
- **Search:** countries and films by title, original title, director or country code.
- **Links everywhere:** every view has its own URL, so Back, Forward and shared links all work.

## How it's built

- **Data at build time:** TMDB's terms forbid redistributing its data, so nothing is committed. Each deploy fetches the catalogue and one details file per film.
- **Performance:** a CPU profile showed three.js re-checking the shaders of 646 translucent country meshes twice every frame. Opaque, pre-mixed colours that look the same removed the work and took the globe from about 23 to 60 fps with the CPU slowed 4×.
- **Design:** a "Velvet Night" look, like a mezzotint print. Everything starts black, and only what has films is lit, in engraved Cinzel capitals and Spectral text.
