Avatar Playground generates avatars in six styles: Flat Cartoon, Pixel Art, Robots, Line Doodle, Monsters and Geometric. Everything runs in the browser.

## What it does

- **Roll and lock:** randomize within a style or across all six, and lock the parts you want to keep.
- **Tweak any part:** every option is previewed on your own avatar.
- **History:** a film strip of your rolls, with undo and redo.
- **Share and export:** a link that recreates the avatar, PNG and SVG downloads, and Copy SVG.
