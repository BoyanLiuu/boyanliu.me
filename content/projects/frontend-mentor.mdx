[Frontend Mentor](https://www.frontendmentor.io) is an online platform that helps web developers build real-world portfolios and practise design-to-code workflows through 120+ coding challenges. Each challenge comes with a professional design and a style guide; the job is to rebuild it faithfully, responsive from mobile to desktop.

## What I practised

- **Layout:** mobile-first responsive pages with Flexbox and CSS Grid, matched against the design files.
- **Interaction:** email validation on the coming-soon and request-access pages, a testimonials slider, an FAQ accordion, a pricing toggle and a light/dark theme switcher.
- **Components and styling at scale:** React with styled-components or Sass, and React Router for the multi-page sites.
- **Shipping:** every build lives in one repository, and most are deployed to Vercel.

## Highlights

The three multi-page sites, **Arch Studio**, **Designo** and the **minimalist portfolio**, were the biggest builds: several routes each, with shared layout components across pages. Browse every build below, with its live site and source code.
